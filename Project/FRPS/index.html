Overview

The goal of the Flexible Robotic Production System was to enable a robot to plan safe, collision-aware motions in a changing workspace. The system combined autonomous motion planning with stereo-camera perception so that real-world objects could be incorporated into the robot's planning environment.

This project was completed as a McMaster University capstone sponsored by Honda of Canada Manufacturing. My work focused on ROS2 and MoveIt2 motion planning, stereo-camera integration, hand-eye calibration, and system testing.

Design & Build

I developed a ROS2 and MoveIt2 pipeline that generated collision-aware trajectories based on an updated representation of the physical workspace. Stereo-camera measurements were transformed into the robot coordinate system through hand-eye calibration.

  • Used MoveIt2 to generate and validate collision-aware robot trajectories
  • Integrated stereo-camera perception to update the planning environment
  • Performed hand-eye calibration and achieved positioning accuracy within 0.2 cm
